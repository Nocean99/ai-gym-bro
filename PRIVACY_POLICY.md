# Privacy Policy for Gym Bro

**Last Updated:** November 15, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Gym Bro ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Gym Bro mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using Gym Bro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you use Gym Bro, we may collect the following types of personal information:

- **Account Information**: Username, email address, and authentication credentials
- **Workout Data**: Exercise logs, sets, reps, weights, workout history, and performance metrics
- **Usage Data**: App interaction patterns, features used, and session duration
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers

### Automatically Collected Information
We automatically collect certain information when you use the app:

- Log data and analytics
- Crash reports and error logs
- Performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### Providing Core Services
- **Workout Tracking**: Store and display your workout history and progress
- **AI Personal Trainer**: Process your workout data to provide personalized recommendations and feedback
- **Performance Analysis**: Analyze your workout patterns to detect personal records and trends
- **Progress Visualization**: Generate charts and statistics based on your workout data

### App Improvement
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Debug and fix technical issues
- Develop new features based on usage patterns
- Optimize AI responses and recommendations

### Communication
- Respond to your support requests and inquiries
- Send important updates about the app (security updates, policy changes)

## AI and Third-Party Services

### OpenAI Integration
Gym Bro uses OpenAI's GPT-4 API to power the conversational AI personal trainer feature. When you interact with the AI trainer:

- Your workout data and conversation messages are sent to OpenAI's servers for processing
- OpenAI processes this data according to their own privacy policy and data usage policies
- We do not control how OpenAI processes or stores this data
- OpenAI's privacy policy can be found at: https://openai.com/privacy

**Data Sent to OpenAI:**
- Your workout logs and exercise history
- Questions and messages you send to the AI trainer
- User preferences and goals

**Important Note:** While we strive to keep your data secure, any information processed by OpenAI is subject to their privacy practices and terms of service.

### Other Third-Party Services
We may use additional third-party services:

- **Analytics Services**: To understand app usage and improve performance
- **Cloud Hosting**: To store and process workout data securely
- **Crash Reporting**: To identify and fix bugs

## Data Storage and Security

### How We Store Your Data
- Workout data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database
- Data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures

### Data Retention
- Active account data is retained as long as your account is active
- You may request deletion of your data at any time
- After account deletion, data is removed within 30 days
- Backups may retain data for up to 90 days for disaster recovery purposes

### Security Measures
While we implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

## Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### Access and Portability
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your workout history and logs

### Correction
- Update or correct inaccurate personal information
- Modify your profile and preferences

### Deletion
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Right to be forgotten (subject to legal obligations)

### Opt-Out
- Disable certain data collection features
- Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the support email provided below.

## Children's Privacy

Gym Bro is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Right to deletion of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

**Note:** We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

**Legal Basis for Processing:**
We process your data based on:
- Your consent when using AI features
- Performance of our service contract with you
- Legitimate interests in improving our service

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.
